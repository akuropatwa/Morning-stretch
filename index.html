How to set up Spotify

You need Spotify Premium. You do this once.

  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
  2. Tap Create app. Name it Morning Stretch and give it any description.
  3. Under Redirect URIs paste https://akuropatwa.github.io/Morning-stretch/ and tap Add.
  4. Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, agree to the terms and tap Save.
  5. Copy the Client ID from the app’s page.
  6. Paste it in the box above and tap Save.
  7. Tap Connect Spotify, log in and agree, then choose a playlist.

Up to five Spotify accounts, yours included, can use your setup. Add them by name and email in the Spotify dashboard under your app’s Settings, User Management.

      Guide

      Get the most from your stretches

      A few minutes each morning, guided by voice. Here is how to make the app work for you.

      Your first morning

      1. 1 Pick a routineShort is about 10 minutes, long about 15.
      2. 2 Score your back1 is sore, 5 is great. Or score it at the end.
      3. 3 Tap Start and lie downPut the phone where you can hear it.

      Every morning

      Your daily stretchThe voice tells you what to do and for how long.Show me howHide
      1. Pick a routine under Routine: short, long or one of your own.
      2. Under the cards you can see its length, voice and music. That is what you will hear.
      3. Score your back from 1 (sore) to 5 (great), now or at the end.
      4. Tap Start.

      The voice names each stretch and says how long it lasts. You get 2 seconds to change position, time calls during each hold, and a word of encouragement at about two minutes in, halfway and near the end.

      During a stretchThe ring and the voice keep time, so you can relax.Show me howHide
      • The ring fills as each stretch goes on. Amber means get ready, green means stretch.
      • Pause stops everything. Back and Skip move between stretches.
      • How pauses the routine and shows the photos and steps.
      • The cross at the top ends the session. It will not count towards today.
      Your recordWatch your streak grow and see how your back is doing.Show me howHide
      • Every day you finish a routine counts, weekends too. Your streak is the number of days in a row.
      • The calendar shows your back score for each day, or a tick if you did not give one. Use the arrows to look back at earlier months.
      • Tap a day to change its back score, add one if you missed it, or clear it. Your streak stays the same.

      Make it yours

      Help me chooseAnswer a few questions and get a routine that suits your back today.Show me howHide

      Tap Help me choose under your routines. It asks where you feel it, what it feels like, what makes it worse, how your back is today, which areas you would like to stretch, what you would like it to help with, where you will stretch, whether you have your strap with you and how long you have. It suggests a routine, says why in plain words, and lists anything it left out on purpose. Save it, or save and start straight away.

      • It starts with a safety check. If any of those signs apply, it does not suggest a routine and tells you who to contact instead.
      • Your answers are remembered, so Check again opens them ready to change and updates the same routine. Your back scores show whether things are getting better.
      • It follows NHS and NICE guidance on back pain and common physiotherapy practice. It is general guidance, not a diagnosis. If things get worse, or have not improved within a few weeks, see a physiotherapist or your GP.
      Create your own routineOne for golf, tennis or a run, from 5 to 30 minutes.Show me howHide
      1. Under Routine, tap Create your own. Tap Next to go on and Back to go back. Not sure what you need? Tap Help me choose on the first screen.
      2. How long? Pick 5, 10, 15, 20, 25 or 30 minutes.
      3. Anything to focus on? Leave it on All-round, or pick one or more parts of the body.
      4. Your stretches. The app picks a balanced set to fit your time. Tap any stretch to see how to do it. Tap Change the stretches to swap, add, remove or move them, then Done.
      5. Which voice? Keep Use my usual voice or pick another. Tap Play to hear one.
      6. Music. Pick what plays during this routine.
      7. Name and save. Give it a name, such as "Before golf", and tap Save.

      Your new routine is chosen straight away and its stretches are in the best order, from lying down to standing. Just tap Start. To delete it, tap Edit, then Delete routine.

      Change a routineSwap, add or move stretches, and change the voice or music.Show me howHide

      Change the stretches

      1. Tap the routine's card, then tap Edit next to its list of stretches.
      2. Swap replaces a stretch with another for the same part of the body.
      3. Up and Down change the order. The cross removes one.
      4. Add a stretch picks more from the library.
      5. Tap Save. The total time shows at the top as you go.

      Reset to the original puts the short or long routine back as it was. Each stretch keeps its own set length.

      Put them in the best order

      After changing the stretches, tap Put in the best order. It keeps the same stretches and only moves them. You see what changed, and Undo puts them back. The button only works when the order would change.

      • You start lying on your back and work up to standing, so you never go back down to the floor.
      • In each position, gentle movement comes first, then moving stretches and strength moves, then the longer holds.
      • Stretches for the same part of the body stay together, and both sides of a stretch stay together.
      • To look after your back, a stretch that bends your lower back forwards is not put first, or straight after another one, when a stretch in the same position can go between.

      The short and long routines keep their own order unless you tap the button on them.

      Change the voice or music

      1. Tap the routine's card.
      2. Under the cards, tap Change voice or music. For your own routines it says Change length, voice or music.
      3. Tap Change next to the thing you want to change, make your choice and tap Done.
      4. Tap Save.

      The short and long routines keep their stretches and timings when you change their voice or music. If you change the length of your own routine, the app picks a new set of stretches. Tap Go back to my old stretches to keep the ones you had.

      Stretch libraryEvery stretch by body part, with photos and clear steps.Show me howHide

      Tap the Stretch library card on the front page. The buttons at the top show one area: lower back, hamstrings, hips and glutes, thighs and calves, upper back and sides, chest, shoulders and neck, or core. Tap any stretch to see the photos, the steps, what you should feel and what to avoid.

      • Most are done lying on the floor. Some are standing or on a chair, which suit the office or a warm-up before golf, tennis or a run.
      • Some build strength rather than stretch, such as the bridge, dead bug, side leg raises, mini squats and calf raises. They should feel like work in the muscles, not in your back.
      • The photos come from a gym library. Where they differ from the steps, a note under the photos says how.

      Sound and music

      Voice and soundChoose a voice you like and hear every word.Show me howHide
      • Tap Sound at the top and tap a voice to hear it. If a routine has its own voice, that voice is used for that routine only.
      • Turn Spoken instructions or Beeps at each change off there too.
      • Turn the volume up and take the phone off silent.
      • With No music the voice plays on its own. On an iPhone, other music stops when the voice starts.
      Stretch to your own musicPlay anything from your music app under the voice.Show me howHide

      Any music app works, Spotify included: Apple Music, YouTube Music, a podcast or the radio too. Tap Sound and choose Keep my music playing.

      • Start your music first, then come back and tap Start.
      • Keep it at a moderate volume. The voice and the music share the phone’s volume.
      • Take the phone off silent. On an iPhone the silent switch mutes the voice but not your music.
      • Tap Test in Sound to check the balance between the voice and your music.
      • A soft chime plays before each instruction. Turn it off in Sound with Soft chime before each instruction.
      • If your music stops when the voice starts, start it again in your music app. It keeps playing from then on.

      Pause pauses the routine, not your music. The routine also pauses if you lock the phone or switch apps. Tap Resume when you are back.

      In the phone app your record is kept on that phone only. Tap Back up or restore, then Copy backup, and paste it into a note or an email to yourself. To restore, paste it into the box and tap Restore. Your own routines are included. Nothing already in the app is overwritten.

      The phone appPut it on your home screen and it opens like any other app.Show me howHide
      • Open https://akuropatwa.github.io/Morning-stretch/ in Safari on an iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
      • It works without a connection once it has been opened online.
      • To get an update, close the app fully and open it again while online. Do it twice if the voices have changed.

      Small things that help

      • The screen stays on while you stretch, so you need not touch the phone.
      • Missed a word? The stretch name, what to do and the count are always on the screen.
      • Tap How during a stretch to see the photos. The routine waits for you.
      • Score your back most days. The calendar then shows how it is going over the weeks.

      Stay safe

      If a stretch sends pain, tingling or numbness further down a leg, stop that one and skip it. Easing back towards the spine is fine. Gentle is better than far. If your back gets worse, check with your physio.

      • Breathe normally and never bounce. A stretch should feel firm, not sharp.
      • Take care with stretches that bend the lower back forwards (knees to chest, single knee to chest, child’s pose), twist it (the lying twist) or pull on the sciatic nerve (the straight-leg hamstring stretches and leg swings). They suit most backs, but a disc can object. Go gently and skip any that bring on symptoms.
      • On a hamstring stretch, if you feel it in your calf or foot, or it tingles, bend the knee a little and relax the ankle.
      • On days when the hamstring stretches pull down the leg, the seated nerve glide is a kinder choice.

      Get medical help straight away if you have new numbness around your bottom or groin, new weakness in a leg, or problems with your bladder or bowels.
