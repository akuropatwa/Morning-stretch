      Morning Stretch

      How it works

      Your daily stretch
      1. Pick a routine under Routine: short (about 10 minutes), long (about 15) or one of your own.
      2. Score your back from 1 (sore) to 5 (great). You can also do this at the end.
      3. Tap Start. Put the phone where you can hear it and lie down.

      The voice names each stretch and says how long it lasts. You get 2 seconds to change position, time calls during each hold, and a word of encouragement at about two minutes in, halfway and near the end.

      During a stretch
      • The ring fills as each stretch goes on. Amber means get ready, green means stretch.
      • Pause stops everything. Back and Skip move between stretches.
      • How pauses the routine and shows the photos and steps.
      • The cross at the top ends the session. It will not count towards today.
      • The screen stays on while you stretch.
      Change a routine
      1. Tap Edit next to the list of stretches.
      2. Use Swap to replace a stretch with another for the same part of the body.
      3. Use Up and Down to change the order, and the cross to remove one.
      4. Tap Add a stretch to pick more from the library.
      5. Tap Save. The new total time shows at the top as you go.

      Reset to the original puts the short or long routine back as it was. Each stretch keeps its own set length.

      Create your own routine
      1. Under Routine, tap Create your own.
      2. Give it a name, for example "Before golf".
      3. Tap Add a stretch for each one you want, then Save.
      4. Tap its card to choose it, then Start.

      To change or delete it, choose it and tap Edit.

      Stretch library

      The Stretch library card lists every stretch by body part: lower back, hamstrings, hips and glutes, thighs and calves, upper back and sides, chest, shoulders and neck, and core. Tap the buttons at the top to show one area. Tap any stretch to see how to do it.

      Voice and sound
      • Tap Sound at the top to choose a voice. Tap one to hear it.
      • Turn spoken instructions or beeps off there too.
      • Turn the volume up and take the phone off silent.
      Music from Spotify

      Your own Spotify playlist can play during the routine. You need Spotify Premium and the phone app version. Music does not work in the Claude version.

      One-off setup

      1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify account.
      2. Tap Create app. Name it Morning Stretch and give it any description.
      3. Under Redirect URIs paste https://akuropatwa.github.io/Morning-stretch/ and tap Add.
      4. Tick Web API and Web Playback SDK, agree to the terms and tap Save.
      5. Copy the Client ID from the app's page.
      6. In this app, tap Sound, turn on Play music from Spotify, paste the Client ID and tap Save.
      7. Tap Connect Spotify, log in and agree, then choose a playlist.

      Using it

      • The music starts with Start, pauses with Pause and stops at the end.
      • Change the playlist any time in Sound.
      • On an iPhone, Spotify does not let other apps change its volume, so the music cannot get quieter while the voice speaks. Pick a calm playlist and set the volume with the phone's buttons.
      • If the music does not start, tap Pause and then Resume.
      • Keep the phone off silent, or the coach's voice may not be heard over the music.
      • Up to five Spotify accounts, including yours, can use your setup. Each needs Spotify Premium, and you add them by name and email in the Spotify dashboard under your app's Settings, User Management.
      Your record
      • Every day you finish a routine counts, weekdays and weekends alike. Your streak is the number of days in a row.
      • The calendar shows your back score for each day, or a tick if you did not give one. Use the arrows to look back at earlier months.
      Back up your record

      In the phone app your record is kept on that phone only. Tap Back up or restore, then Copy backup, and paste it into a note or an email to yourself. To restore, paste it into the box and tap Restore. Your own routines are included. Nothing already in the app is overwritten.

      The phone app
      • Install it from https://akuropatwa.github.io/Morning-stretch/. On an iPhone use Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
      • It works without a connection once it has been opened online. Music needs a connection.
      • To get an update, close the app fully and open it again while online. Do it twice if the voices have changed.
      Stay safe

      If a stretch sends pain, tingling or numbness further down a leg, stop that one and skip it. Easing back towards the spine is fine. Gentle is better than far. If your back gets worse, check with your physio.
